Can You Spot a Phishing Attack?

Learn how to identify suspicious emails, links, and social engineering techniques before they become a security threat.

🎣

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a social engineering technique where attackers attempt to trick people into revealing sensitive information or performing unsafe actions.

🔗

Suspicious Links

Attackers may use deceptive links that lead users to fraudulent websites.

🚨

Urgent Messages

Attackers often create urgency or fear to pressure users into acting quickly.

👤

Impersonation

Attackers may impersonate trusted people, companies, or services.

KNOW THE THREATS

Common Phishing Techniques

Understanding common phishing techniques can help you recognize suspicious messages and avoid unsafe actions.

01

Email Phishing

Fraudulent emails are designed to look like legitimate communications from organizations, services, or individuals.

EMAIL
02

Spear Phishing

A targeted phishing attempt designed for a specific person or organization using personalized information.

TARGETED
03

Smishing

Phishing attempts delivered through SMS or other messaging services.

SMS
04

Vishing

Social engineering attacks carried out through voice calls or phone conversations.

VOICE
05

Clone Phishing

A legitimate message may be copied or imitated and modified to contain a malicious or deceptive link.

CLONED
06

Social Engineering

Attackers manipulate trust, urgency, fear, or curiosity to influence a person's actions.

STAY SAFE

How to Protect Yourself

Follow these simple practices to reduce your risk of falling victim to phishing attacks.

🔗

Check Links

Hover over links and verify the destination before clicking.

📧

Verify the Sender

Check the sender's address carefully, especially for unexpected messages.

🔐

Protect Credentials

Never share passwords, verification codes, or sensitive information through suspicious messages.

⏸️

Stop and Think

Be cautious when a message creates urgency or pressure you to act immediately.

🛡️

Use Security Tools

Keep your browser, operating system, and security software updated.

🚨

Report Phishing

Report suspicious messages through your organization's approved security channel.
